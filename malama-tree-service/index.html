A Real Call

When a banyan came down on three cars.

During a Kona storm, a large banyan tree came down onto three parked vehicles. According to the customer who called Mālama Tree Service in, the crew worked carefully to cut back the tree and relieve the pressure on the vehicles — limiting further damage rather than making it worse.

It's the kind of job that doesn't leave room for guesswork: a heavy tree, vehicles underneath, and a homeowner who needed it handled safely and quickly.

About

Local tree care, care taken seriously.

Mālama Tree Service works on properties in and around Keaau, Hawaii — handling everything from routine trimming to storm-damage cleanup. The name comes from mālama, a Hawaiian word often understood as care, tending, or stewardship — reflected in how the reviews describe the work: careful, communicative, and done right the first time.

Darrick DeAnda's name is on the company's own Google listing alongside the trimming work — the same Darrick customers mention by name in their reviews.
