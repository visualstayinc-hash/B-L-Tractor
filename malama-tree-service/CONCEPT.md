# Mālama Tree Service — Concept

**Business:** Mālama Tree Service, Keaau, HI 96749, (808) 640-5035
**Google:** 5.0★, 17 reviews, "Tree service" category, Open 24 hours

## Constraint that shaped this build

The real supplied tree-work photos/video (a flyer graphic, then 3 real job-site
photos) were never accessible as embeddable files in this build environment —
confirmed repeatedly via filesystem search. Rather than fabricate "real"
photography or closely trace the supplied images without a clean way to embed
them, the site uses an **original illustrated visual identity** (SVG canopy
scenes, an illustrated before/after transformation, an illustrated storm-story
graphic) built from the palette and shapes actually observed in the supplied
images, honestly labeled as illustration where it matters (the transformation
slider caption says "illustrated representation, not a specific job-site
photo"). All *text* facts — services, reviews, rating, phone, location, hours,
the storm story — come directly from the client-supplied brief and are not
fabricated.

## Brand thesis

Mālama (Hawaiian: care / tending / stewardship) — care for the tree, the
property, the community. Hawaii as working context, not tourist cliché: no
hibiscus, tiki, cocktails, or postcard beaches. The trees and the work are the
visual identity.

## Visual language

Deep forest green / charcoal / earth brown base, warm cream paper, a muted
amber accent (equipment/safety tone) for CTAs. Fraunces (organic display
serif) for headlines, Inter for body — bold but readable.

## Signature interaction

Drag-to-reveal "Overgrown → Cared-for" transformation slider (keyboard
accessible via arrow keys), matching the brief's "tree transformation"
request using original illustrated before/after scenes.

## Facts never fabricated

No invented prices, service-area street address, certifications, years in
business, staff size, equipment beyond what's named, or review count/rating
beyond the supplied 5.0★/17. "Darrick" is referenced only inside the verbatim
review quotes where customers named him — never asserted as "the owner"
anywhere on the site.
