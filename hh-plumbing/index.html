About H&H

Amarillo plumbers. Not a franchise.

H&H Plumbing & Mechanical is a local contractor at 117 N Polk St in downtown Amarillo. We hold plumbing licenses with the State of Texas and the City of Amarillo, and we've been working on the Panhandle's water, gas and sewer lines for decades.

We handle the little repairs and the big ones, and we're straight with you about which one you've got. If a drain just needs clearing, that's what we'll do. If the sewer line is gone, we'll show you why before you spend a dime on it.
