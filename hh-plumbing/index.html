Before we get there

What to do while you wait

A few minutes of the right move can save you a ruined floor. Do this, then call.

In Amarillo it's usually at the meter box near the street or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then open a low faucet to drain pressure off the line, and call us.

Leave the building, don't flip switches, don't light anything. Call your gas utility's emergency line or 911 from outside. Once it's safe, call us to find and repair the leak.

Every flush and load of laundry comes up the lowest drain in the house. Stop running water anywhere and call. If you have an outdoor cleanout, tell us where it is.

Gas units: check if the pilot light is out. Electric units: check the breaker. If either looks fine and there's still no hot water, the tank likely needs service — tell us gas or electric when you call.
