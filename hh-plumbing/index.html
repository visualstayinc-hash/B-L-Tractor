Before we get there

Three things to dowhile you wait.

A few minutes of the right move can save you a ruined floor. Do these, then call us.

Water leak? Shut off the main.

In Amarillo it's usually at the meter box near the street or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then open a low faucet to drain pressure off the line.

Smell gas? Get out first.

Leave the building, don't flip switches, don't light anything. Call your gas utility's emergency line or 911 from outside. Once it's safe, call us to find and repair the leak.

Sewer backing up? Stop using water.

Every flush and every load of laundry is going to come up the lowest drain in the house. Stop running water anywhere and call. If you have an outdoor cleanout, tell us where it is.
