03 — Before we get there

What to do in the first five minutes.

Most damage happens while people look for the phone number. Do these first, then call.

Swipe or use the arrows

Burst pipe

Find the main shut-off, usually near the meter at the street or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Open a low faucet to drain the line.

Shut off main → then call

No hot water

Check the breaker for electric units or the pilot light for gas. If there's water pooling under the tank, shut off the cold supply valve on top of the heater.

Check power → shut cold valve

Sewer backup

Stop running water everywhere in the house. Don't flush. Keep people and pets away from any standing water until the line is cleared.

Stop all water use

Gas smell

Don't flip switches or light anything. Leave the building, then call the gas company from outside. Call us once the utility has made it safe.

Leave first · call from outside

Panhandle cold snap

When the wind drops it below 20°F, let a faucet on an exterior wall drip and open the cabinet doors under sinks. A frozen line found early is a repair, not a flood.

Drip · open cabinets · call
