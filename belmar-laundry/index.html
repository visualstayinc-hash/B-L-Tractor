Good to know

Questions we get at the counter.

How does drop-off work?

Bring your laundry in, we weigh it at the counter, and you pay by the pound. We separate darks and whites, wash, dry, and fold. Most orders are ready the next day.

Do I need to bring quarters?

No. There's a coin changer on site, so bring bills and the attendant or the changer can break them for you.

Can I wash comforters and blankets?

Yes. The larger washers handle comforters, blankets, and other bulky items that don't fit in a home machine.

Is there somewhere to sit and wait?

Yes. Seating, free Wi-Fi, and an attendant on site. Plenty of people work or study while their loads run.

Do you do dry cleaning or pickup and delivery?

Ask the attendant or call ahead. Some listings show these options, and availability can vary.

Before you come in

A few tips that save a trip.

Empty the pockets

Pens, receipts, and lip balm cause more ruined loads than anything else.

Bring bills

The changer handles the quarters. You don't need to raid the coin jar.

Mornings are quietest

Doors open at 7 AM. Early loads mean short waits for dryers.

Drop it off on the way to work.

Pick it up folded on the way home.
