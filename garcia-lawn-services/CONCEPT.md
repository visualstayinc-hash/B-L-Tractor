# Concept — Garcia Lawn Services

**Thesis:** From Overgrown to Clean. The lack of client photography is treated as the reason to build a stronger interaction/visual system, not an excuse for a generic template.

**Visual system:** grass blades (generated, not stock), mowing lines, irrigation lines/drops, mowing-stripe fills, yard-boundary geometry. Palette: deep/mid/light living green, warm off-white, dark charcoal, restrained water blue.

**Structure:**
1. Hero — static, identity + CTA visible immediately (ambient swaying grass behind it).
2. Transform section — dedicated pinned scroll sequence: Overgrown → Cutting → Edged & Clean (mow-line sweep, height shrink, mowing stripes fade in, one water pulse).
3. The Difference — signature illustrated (not photographic) before/after drag slider, keyboard- and touch-accessible via a native range input. Explicitly captioned as illustrated, with the frame built to accept real photography later.
4. Services — Lawn Care / Irrigation / Yard Care selector driving a shared SVG yard diagram, not card grid.
5. Reviews — real supplied Google reviews (4.8★/53), used verbatim, truncation and photo-reference notes disclosed honestly.
6. Quote — chip-select yard need → call CTA (no dead-end form, since there's no backend to submit to).
7. Contact — phone, "serves its local area" (no invented address), no fabricated hours.

**Facts discipline:** no address, no fabricated weekly hours, no service claims beyond mowing/irrigation/yard care, no invented reviews, no photos claimed as Garcia's actual work.
