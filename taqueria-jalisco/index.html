The wall out front

Painted, not printed.

Jalisco is the state the agave plant calls home — tequila, mariachi, the food this menu is built on. Lubbock taquerías carry that identity the way they always have: a sign painted by hand, papel picado strung for a birthday, color that doesn't fade to a corporate palette. This is our version of that wall, drawn for the page.

Original illustration, not a photograph of the real storefront — we didn't have a verified photo of the actual mural to work from, so this is drawn in the same spirit rather than presented as the real thing.
